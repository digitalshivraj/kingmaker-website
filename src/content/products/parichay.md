## What it did

For a government school ID card contract in Bihar, Parichay collected and verified data for students across 20 districts, checked every photo with an AI quality-control step, and produced print packs that went straight to the press, one PDF per school.

## The pipeline

1. Import the department's student data in its existing format
2. Agents visit schools and capture photos with a fast phone app
3. Automatic matching of photos to records, with manual review where needed
4. AI quality check flags blurred photos, wrong framing and duplicate records
5. Cards generated in the approved format and packed per school for printing
6. Department sees school-wise and district-wise completion live

## Reusable for any drive

The same system works for employee ID cards, certificates, voter slips, health cards and any job where thousands of records and photos must become printed documents without errors.
