## Why small hospitals do not have software

Hospital software is usually priced for corporate hospitals and takes months to set up. A 30-bed hospital in a taluka town runs on registers and a billing computer.

This system is priced as a small monthly subscription, runs on phones and existing computers, and a hospital can be onboarded in a day: we create the hospital login, the owner adds doctors and staff with their roles, and OPD starts the same afternoon.

## Modules

- OPD: tokens, queue display, consultation notes, prescriptions
- Pharmacy: stock, sales against prescriptions, expiry alerts
- Lab: orders, results, report delivery on WhatsApp
- Billing: OPD, IPD and pharmacy bills, GST, daily and monthly reports
- IPD: admission, bed board, nursing notes, discharge summary
- Admin: staff roles, doctor schedules, hospital settings

## Patient booking on WhatsApp

Many clinics do not have a Meta business page or WhatsApp Business account. The platform runs one WhatsApp number that every onboarded hospital can use for bookings and queue updates, so patients get a token and a reminder without the clinic setting anything up.
