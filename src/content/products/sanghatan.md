## Built from a real organisation plan

Sanghatan was built around a 30-day organisational plan covering every Vidhan Sabha constituency in Maharashtra: booth committees, new members, new shakhas, a digital party fund, scheme beneficiary outreach and monthly janta darbars. The software tracks each of those targets at every level of the organisation.

## War room

- Live numbers for members, booths covered, shakhas opened and fund collected
- Drill down from state to division to Lok Sabha to Vidhan Sabha to booth
- Leaderboards for pramukhs and karyakartas
- Daily MIS report to the leadership

## Karyakarta app

A mobile app in Marathi and English that ground workers actually use: a social feed with official posts, reels and stories, member registration with receipts, party fund collection by UPI, a grievance and meeting-request chatbot for voters, a profile with rank and QR identity card, and forms the office can push to any wing.

## Voter data done properly

Voter list handling is built around Election Commission rules and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, with access limited by post and no bulk download for lower levels.
