## What the client needed

A packers and movers company with a head office in Bihar and franchises in several cities wanted the kind of panel that national courier companies give their partners: book a shipment, see where it is, settle accounts, all from a branch login.

## What was built

- Head office super admin with every booking, every franchise and every rupee
- Franchise panel for bookings, pickups, status updates and daily reports
- Customer tracking page linked from the company website and WhatsApp
- Rate cards and invoices generated per booking
- Roadmap for hub operations (mother hub, bag-level scanning, route sheets)

## Hosting and handover

The platform runs on the client's own virtual server in Mumbai with a full handover document, so the company is never locked in.
