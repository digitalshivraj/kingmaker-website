## What an MLA office deals with every day

Hundreds of calls and visits: a road not repaired, a water connection, a name missing from a ration card, a request to attend a function. Most are noted on paper or in a WhatsApp group and many are forgotten.

JanSeva gives the office one place for all of it. Every request has an owner, a department and a status. The citizen gets a message when it moves. The MLA sees which departments are slow and which wards raise the most complaints.

## Citizen side

- Simple Marathi app that opens from a link, no app store download
- Login by mobile number with OTP
- Raise a complaint with a photo and location
- Request an appointment, attend an event, ask about a government scheme
- See the status of everything they raised

## Office side

- Dashboard with new, in-progress and closed requests
- Assign to departments (PWD, water supply, revenue, police and more) and set follow-up dates
- Ward-wise and department-wise reports
- Broadcast lists for events and announcements, with consent records

## Nagar Parishad variant

The same system with citizen services for a municipal council: complaints by ward, tax and certificate queries, and a public notice board.
