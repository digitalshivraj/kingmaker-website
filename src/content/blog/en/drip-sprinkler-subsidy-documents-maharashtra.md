A farmer in Jalgaon gets the message he has been waiting for: his sprinkler application has been selected. The portal now wants his documents. His 7/12 extract is from last year, the passbook photo is blurred, and his Aadhaar card carries a middle name that the land record does not.

The dealer who filed the application sees the same thing in file after file. In a typical case the farmer is eligible and the set is ready in the godown. What holds up the subsidy is paper.

## Where you apply decides the list

Drip and sprinkler subsidy in Maharashtra comes through more than one scheme. The state agriculture department's schemes are filed on the [MahaDBT farmer portal](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/). Farmers in villages covered by PoCRA, the Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani project, apply on the [PoCRA DBT portal](https://dbt.mahapocra.gov.in/).

Each scheme publishes its own document list, and the list can change from one year to the next. Treat what follows as **the usual set**, not the final word.

## The documents usually asked for

Keep these ready before the application is filed, not after selection.

- **Land record**: recent 7/12 and 8-A extracts showing the applicant's name and the land where the set will go.
- Aadhaar: the card, with a linked mobile number the farmer still uses, because portals commonly verify by OTP.
- Bank details: the first page of the passbook with name, account number and IFSC. The subsidy is credited straight to the account, so it should be active and linked to Aadhaar.
- Category proof: the relevant certificate, if the farmer applies under a reserved category.
- Others the scheme specifies: for example a consent letter from co-holders, a declaration about the water source, or the dealer's quotation.

How conditional the list can be shows on PoCRA's own [sprinkler irrigation page](https://dbt.mahapocra.gov.in/Home/ActivityList/19?Name=Sprinkler+Irrigation). Beyond the 7/12 and 8-A, two of its items depend on who the farmer is (proof of Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe category, a disability certificate), and a third, a written agreement from the other users, comes in only when the water source is shared.

Farmers get caught on the crop entry too. PoCRA's eligibility notes link the sprinkler set to a crop recorded with its area on the 7/12, with a planting certificate from the agriculture supervisor as the fallback.

## Make the names match across every document

A name spelled three ways across three documents is a typical reason for a file to come back.

1. Lay the Aadhaar card, 7/12, 8-A and passbook side by side.
2. Compare the first name, the father's or husband's name and the surname, in Marathi and in English.
3. If one document differs, ask which office corrects it and start that correction first.
4. If the land is jointly held or still in a parent's name, ask the taluka agriculture office what it accepts **before anything is purchased**.

## Keep clear scans on the phone

A scan taken properly once saves a second trip from the farm to the shop.

- Place the page flat in daylight, with all four corners showing and no shadow or fingers on it.
- Use the phone's document scan mode, then zoom in to check that survey and account numbers can be read.
- Save in the format and file size shown on the portal's upload screen.
- Name each file with the farmer's surname and the document.

## After selection: bill upload and inspection

The first upload is not the last. Once the application is selected and sanctioned, the farmer buys the set and a second round of paper begins. It usually includes:

- The dealer's tax invoice with GST, in the farmer's name exactly as on the application.
- Proof of payment in the form the scheme accepts.
- The design or layout of the system prepared by the company or dealer.

Ask the agriculture office **before buying**. In these schemes the purchase normally follows pre-sanction, and a bill dated earlier can create trouble.

Checking does not stop at the bill. The central [Per Drop More Crop guidelines](https://cdp.nhb.gov.in/cdp_docs/PDMC%20guidlines.pdf) leave no farm out of the field check, and the checkers come from outside agencies hired by the state. How the visit is arranged in your taluka is for the agriculture office to say. Keep the originals and the invoice at hand that day.

## Confirm the current list before you file

Open the scheme's page on the official portal, or ask the taluka agriculture office, and write down **the current list** for your scheme and category.

Dealers in Parbhani, Nashik or Amravati can keep a printed checklist for each scheme at the counter and revise it every season. ## Common questions

### Can I apply if the land is in my father's name?

Usually not on your own, because the land record is expected to be in the applicant's name. Schemes differ on what they accept for joint or inherited land, so ask the taluka agriculture office before you file.

### Do I upload the dealer's bill with the application?

No, the bill normally comes later, after selection and pre-sanction. The application stage is about identity, land and bank documents, though some schemes ask for a quotation.

### What if my name is spelled differently on Aadhaar and the 7/12?

Get it corrected before you apply, wherever that is possible. The office that fixes it depends on which document is wrong, so ask the agriculture office what it will accept while the correction is in progress.
