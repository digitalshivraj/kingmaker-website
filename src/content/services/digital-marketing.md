## Ten years of running ads

Our team has run campaigns for an aesthetic clinic chain, packers and movers, a Birmingham cleaning company and e-commerce stores. The lesson from all of them: clicks are cheap, qualified leads are not. We track every lead from the ad to the call and cut what does not convert.

## Lead quality first

Most lead campaigns fail at the follow-up, not the ad. We add a WhatsApp qualification step so the sales team calls only people who confirmed their interest, location and need. Our Kasauti lead firewall product is being built around exactly this.

## What a month looks like

1. Set up tracking, landing pages and WhatsApp flows
2. Launch and test creatives and audiences
3. Review cost per qualified lead every week and move budget
4. Report in plain numbers: spend, leads, qualified leads, bookings
