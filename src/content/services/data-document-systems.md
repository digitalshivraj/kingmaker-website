## Thousands of records, zero typos

When a department has to produce forty thousand ID cards, or a business has to reconcile a year of payments for its accountant, doing it by hand means errors. We build pipelines that take the data once and produce every document correctly.

## What we have built

- Parichay: state-scale ID card generation with AI quality checks and per-school print PDFs
- A Marathi ID card generator for an NGO's members
- A PDF editor that changes text inside existing PDFs while keeping the original font, with bulk find and replace
- Voter roll and contact data matching across 8 districts
- Payment and party ledger with Tally export (in planning)

## Formats

Excel and CSV in, PDF, print-ready sheets, Tally XML and clean spreadsheets out.
