## Why WhatsApp

Your citizens, patients and customers already use it every day. A bot that answers in Marathi at 9 pm, confirms a token, or tells a farmer that the subsidy file has moved does more for satisfaction than any app download.

## What we have built

- An NGO chatbot for scheme help, member registration and volunteers, with a Marathi ID card generated and sent back
- Farmer updates at every stage of a subsidy application
- Booking and queue messages for clinics
- Lead qualification flows for ad campaigns, so sales teams call only real enquiries

## The official way

We build on the Meta Cloud API with proper business verification, templates and opt-ins. No grey-market tools that get numbers banned. We also build in-house automation (n8n and custom code) so you are not tied to a monthly per-seat tool unless you want one.
