## Data that must not leak

A lodge register, a patient record, a voter list: these systems are only acceptable if the data is protected. We design every system with roles, logs and retention rules from the first day, and we review systems built by others.

## What a review covers

- Who can read and write each table, and whether the database enforces it
- Exposed API keys, public storage buckets and default passwords
- Login strength, OTP flows and session handling
- Backup and restore, and what happens when a phone is lost
- Consent, purpose and retention under the DPDP Act, 2023

## Experience

We have locked down production databases after a security advisor flagged exposed tables, rotated compromised keys, and written the access models for police, political and healthcare systems.
