## What we build

Most of our products started as custom software for one client: a lodge register for police, a subsidy tracker for a dealer, a franchise panel for a packers company, a back-office CRM for a broker. We build the system around your real process, not around a template.

## How a project runs

1. We sit with your team (in person anywhere in Maharashtra, on call elsewhere) and write down the process, the roles and the reports.
2. You get a working demo on a free link within the first phase, so you can click through it before paying for a full build.
3. We build in stages, each stage usable on its own.
4. We deploy on a server you control, or one we manage for you, and hand over documentation.

## Technology

Modern web stacks (Next.js, Astro, vanilla JavaScript), Postgres databases with row-level security, hosted in Indian regions, with WhatsApp and payment integrations where needed. No lock-in: you own the code and the data.
