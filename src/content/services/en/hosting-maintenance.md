## Your server, your data

We deploy on servers you own (Hostinger, Hetzner, AWS Mumbai, or a government cloud) with full access handed over. If you want us to manage it, we do, with you keeping the keys.

## What we handle

- Ubuntu servers with panels (aaPanel, Coolify) or plain Docker
- Databases with daily backups and tested restores
- SSL certificates, domains and email setup
- Automation servers (n8n) for workflows
- Security basics: row-level security, access logs, key rotation

## Start free, pay when it is proven

For new projects we host the demo on free tiers and share only a link. You move to a paid server after you approve the build, usually a few hundred rupees a month for a small system.
