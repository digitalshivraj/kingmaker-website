## Design that gets read

A proposal to a district SP, a deck for party leadership, a brochure for hospital owners: each has to look credible at first glance and be easy to read in Marathi. We design the document around the reader, with correct Devanagari type and a clean layout.

## What we have made

- Brand identity for a packers and movers business and a food brand
- Party-neutral and party-specific election decks in Marathi
- Government proposal letters and letterheads
- Product brochures and demo guides for software pilots
- Our own identity: the Kingmaker shield and crown

## Working with developers in the same team

Because the designers and developers sit together, the brand carries through to the software, the website and the WhatsApp templates without a handover.
