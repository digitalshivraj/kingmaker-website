## From plan to platform

Party organisation plans are ambitious: committees at every booth, lakhs of new members, shakhas in every village, a digital fund. Without software, the plan lives in WhatsApp groups and nobody knows the real numbers.

We turn the plan into a live system where each level sees its own targets and the leadership sees the whole state.

## What we have built

- Sanghatan: war room dashboard, booth wall and a full karyakarta social app, demonstrated to party leadership and an election technology company
- Chanakya: campaign cadre app with daily content, birthday cards, DP frames and panna-wise voter lists
- Padvidhar voter app for the graduates constituency roll across 8 districts
- JanSeva: citizen services for MLA offices and Nagar Parishads
- Marathi pitch decks for two major Maharashtra parties

## Rules we build to

Election Commission guidelines on voter data, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, WhatsApp terms for broadcasts, and role-based access so no lower-level user can download bulk voter data.
