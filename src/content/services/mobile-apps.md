## Apps that work in a village

A field worker in a taluka has a mid-range Android phone, patchy 4G and no time for a complicated screen. Our apps capture data offline, sync when there is signal, and keep every screen to a few taps in Marathi.

## What we have built

- Campaign cadre apps with feeds, member registration and booth tracking
- Photo capture apps that collected tens of thousands of student photos for ID cards
- A citizen grievance app for MLA offices
- A patient token and queue app for doctors
- A medication companion app for seniors

## PWA or native

For most projects a progressive web app is the right choice: no app store delay, opens from a WhatsApp link, installs to the home screen. We build native apps with React Native when the app needs deep device access or a store presence.
