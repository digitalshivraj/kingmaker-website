## How we work with government

We are a registered micro enterprise (Udyam), GST registered, with a Maharashtra Shop and Establishment registration. We prepare the proposal in Marathi, present it to the department head, run a pilot at no cost so the office can see the system working with its own data, and only then move to a work order or a GeM purchase.

## Current government work

- Maha-Stay 360, a digital lodge register for district police, with proposals submitted to SP offices in Marathwada
- Parichay, student ID card data collection and printing for PM SHRI schools in Bihar
- Social media and rumour monitoring for district police, in development
- Citizen service dashboards for MLA offices and Nagar Parishads

## GeM

Government buyers can procure our software development, web portal, data processing and maintenance services through the Government e-Marketplace. See the Government page for details and documents.

## Compliance

Every system follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, keeps data inside India, logs every access and gives the department ownership of the data and the code.
